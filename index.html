Aswan University · Training

Training: How to Create Products That Actually Sell?

I recently delivered a two-day training at Aswan University, aimed at showing how to turn any idea into a product that can actually enter the market and sell. We worked through the full journey in a practical way: starting with really understanding Customer Needs, and the importance of Market Research and Idea Validation before any execution step. Then we moved on to precisely defining the Target Audience, building a clear Value Proposition, along with Competitor Analysis and understanding where the product stands in the market. We also talked about types of markets, especially the difference between a Red Ocean and a Blue Ocean: how some markets face high competition and price pressure, while others offer a bigger opportunity to create different value away from the crowd — and when to choose each path depending on your idea. One of the key points we focused on was the idea of an MVP (Minimum Viable Product): starting with a simple version of the product, testing the market quickly, and learning before investing time and money into something that isn't guaranteed to work. We also covered Go-To-Market Strategy, choosing the right Sales & Distribution Channels, and how Customer Experience can be the factor that separates a product that lasts from one that gets forgotten. #training #BusinessDevelopment #valueproposition

ILO × Microsoft — Women in Digital Business

Training of Trainers (TOT) Program

Last week was a very special experience for me. I successfully completed the Training of Trainers (TOT) program with WIDB (Women in Digital Business). The training took place over 5 full days – 25 hours in total, and was implemented by the International Labour Organization (ILO) in collaboration with Microsoft. It was a great learning journey where I explored how to deliver information in a practical and simple way, and how to engage with different types of participants while keeping the sessions interactive and full of energy. I also had the chance to give a short presentation on Handling & Shipping, which opened an important discussion about the challenges in this field and possible ways to deal with them. A big thank you to our trainer, Mohammed Abd El Aaty, for his effort, guidance, and the valuable experiences he shared with us. His energy throughout the program made a real difference. #ILO #Microsoft #TOT
